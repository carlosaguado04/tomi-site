Privacy

This website doesn't collect anything about you. One button on it talks to someone else, and here's what that means.

Last updated: November 6, 2026

What this site doesn't do

There are no analytics, no cookies, no ads and no forms. The site itself doesn't collect personal data. The fonts, images and the Tomi animation are served from this site, not from anyone else.

What stays in your browser

If you switch between the light and dark theme, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage under tomi-theme, so the site remembers it next time. It never leaves your browser. You can clear it by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare may record technical details about requests in its server logs, such as your IP address and browser user agent.

The Ko-fi button

The footer of the home page has a “Support me on Ko-fi” button. It's a widget from Ko-fi: when the page loads, your browser fetches a script and images from storage.ko-fi.com, and Ko-fi's script also loads a font from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com). That means Ko-fi and Google can receive your IP address and browser details.

How they handle that is covered by Ko-fi's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy. If you click the button, you go to ko-fi.com, which is Ko-fi's own site. This page doesn't load the widget.
