Work, then rest.

When a work session ends, your screen fades out with one quiet line from Tomi. Click or press Esc to get back in. Quick timers never blur your screen, and you can turn the break screen off in Settings.

  • Take a break.
  • Back to it.
  • Long break. Unclench.

Tomi can nudge you to drink water and rest your eyes. Both are off until you turn them on.

  • Water: every 30, 45, 60 or 90 minutes, as a quiet nudge or a break screen.
  • Rest your eyes: every 15, 20 or 30 minutes.

Need a plain timer? Say it.

Say it.

Just say what you need. Type "Cook pasta for 20 minutes, then call my mom in 45 minutes", or press ⌃⌥V and speak it. Press Return and the timers start. Turn on Autostart and spoken timers start on their own.

Sentences need Apple Intelligence; without it, type a time like 25m.

Or pull the thread.

Pull down from the smile. The longer you pull, the longer the timer. Let go, name it if you like, and press Start.

Keeps your Mac awake, only as long as you need.

Until you turn it off, for two lengths you set in Settings (1 and 2 hours to start), or only during a Pomodoro.
